# Art-direction worksheet

Complete this worksheet before a major visual redesign. The direction is a
proposal until a human approves it.

## Product lens

- Audience: developers evaluating a small experimental frontend framework.
- Primary job: understand a capability, open a runnable example, and inspect
  credible evidence of the behavior.
- Surface mode: the landing page is Persuade/Experience; application pages are
  Operate; technical explanations are Read.
- Tone: technically confident, curious, restrained, and honest about alpha
  status.

## Direction contract

Write one sentence for the visual thesis and name the ownable visual world.
Avoid defaulting to “modern SaaS,” “clean tech,” or a color trend. State what
the first viewport should make the visitor notice and do.

## System decisions

Record deliberate choices for:

- typography roles, type scale, line lengths, and code/data treatment;
- color roles, contrast ratios, status colors, and experimental-status signal;
- spacing unit, container widths, control heights, radii, borders, and shadows;
- page hierarchy, navigation, section rhythm, and example-to-source paths;
- component states: hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, error, empty, and
  loading where applicable;
- responsive behavior at the tested desktop and mobile viewports.

Prefer a small coherent system over a pile of one-off values. Every visual
element should explain product structure, support a task, or establish a
specific Ruseact point of view.

## Reference research record

Use at least two real references when proposing a new world. Record the URL,
surface observed, useful transferable principle, and what will explicitly not
be copied. References are evidence for decisions, not mood-board decoration.
